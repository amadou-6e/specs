# Research method

## Questions

List the bounded questions this research can answer.

## Search strategy

Record search dates, repositories, databases, queries, browsing paths, inclusion and
exclusion criteria, and any language or access restrictions.

## Inspection method

Distinguish reading published descriptions from inspecting source code, downloading
artifacts, reproducing commands, or measuring behavior directly.

## Evaluation method

Define comparison dimensions, evidence classes, confidence criteria, and how
conflicting sources are handled before synthesizing conclusions.

## Limitations

Record incomplete access, uninspected artifacts, temporal limits, selection bias, and
questions the method cannot resolve.

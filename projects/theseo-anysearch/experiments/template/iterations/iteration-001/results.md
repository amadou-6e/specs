# Results

Report primary and per-seed results, uncertainty, baseline comparisons, passed and
failed gates, negative findings, unexpected observations, interpretation, non-claims,
and the disposition dictated by the protocol. Link to raw evidence instead of copying
large reports into prose.

Give every preregistered gate an explicit `passed`, `failed`, or `unevaluable`
disposition. Separate direct measurements from derived statistics and interpretation.
If the team chooses an action different from the protocol's required disposition,
link the separate decision rather than rewriting this result.

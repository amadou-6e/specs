One evidenced set of agreement terms effective for a period of one Liability. A renegotiation starts a new Liability term without changing the Liability identity.

The relationship is optional: a Liability can be known from a statement before its agreement is available. A term describes contractual conditions, not a dated outstanding balance or a posted repayment.

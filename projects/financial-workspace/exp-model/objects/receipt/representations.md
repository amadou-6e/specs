# Receipt forms

Email body, PDF attachment, standalone PDF, image, scan or structured employer export. Payslip PDFs and employer exports are income-statement Receipts. Document kind is independent of format. An invoice does not prove payment; one email can contain several receipts.

Follow the [shared representation contract](../../representations/contract.md) for native IDs, source locations, field assertions, hashes, extraction versions and review states.

## Declared associations

Direct forms: email, pdf, image.

Evidence forms: See the direct representations.

Metadata owns these associations. A source document identifying an account does not become the account itself.

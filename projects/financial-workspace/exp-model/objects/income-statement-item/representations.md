# Income statement item evidence forms

Items are identified within PDF payslips, scanned statements or structured employer
exports. They inherit the parent's document context and retain their own
representation ID and page/row locator. Metadata declares supporting evidence
forms rather than treating each line as an independent document.

Use the [provenance contract](../../representations/provenance.yaml).
Preserve OCR uncertainty, original labels and signs. Repeated labels are not
sufficient to identify or deduplicate a line.

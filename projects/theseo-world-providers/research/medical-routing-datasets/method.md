# Research method

## Questions

- Does each dataset provide a surface and an explicit path-like centerline?
- Are their coordinate systems and physical units explicitly documented?
- What access, rights, or conversion constraints limit use?

## Search and inspection method

This registry extracts source characterizations from the existing dataset survey.
That survey reviewed dataset records, descriptions, repositories, representative
files, formats, and reported licenses. This migration does not repeat those checks.

## Evaluation method

Keep published availability separate from directly inspected content. Treat missing
coordinate statements as unspecified, not inferred. Distinguish supplied centerlines
from centerlines proposed for later extraction.

## Limitations

The originating survey did not record one uniform access date or immutable revision
for every source. Freshness, rights, payload hashes, and route/surface alignment must
be revalidated before experimental use.

# S-004 — AneuX morphology database

## Material

- Canonical URL: [Zenodo record](https://zenodo.org/records/6678442)
- Source type: aneurysm morphology database
- Evidence class: primary
- Accessed: not uniformly recorded in the originating survey
- Rights: CC BY-NC 4.0 reported by the originating survey

## Relevance and claims used

The dataset description reports a large collection of surface meshes and centerlines
stored in VTP or STL-related forms, making it a possible geometry/reference corpus.

## Material inspected

The Zenodo content description was reviewed; this migration did not inspect every
payload member.

## Limitations

Coordinate semantics, per-case route meaning, standardized endpoints, and
collision-evaluation labels were not established in the inspected material.

# S-003 — ASOCA

## Material

- Canonical links: [data record](https://reshare.ukdataservice.ac.uk/855916/), [description](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2211.01859), [support repository](https://github.com/Ramtingh/ASOCADataDescription)
- Source type: coronary CT challenge dataset
- Evidence class: primary
- Accessed: not uniformly recorded in the originating survey
- Rights: UK Data Service End User Licence reported for downloads

## Relevance and claims used

The survey reports STL surface meshes and VTP centerlines for 40 training cases, with
additional CT volume and segmentation material.

## Material inspected

The dataset description, access record, and support repository were reviewed.

## Limitations

Access is restricted and the inspected text did not explicitly state centerline world
coordinates. Challenge segmentation metrics are not routing-task metrics.

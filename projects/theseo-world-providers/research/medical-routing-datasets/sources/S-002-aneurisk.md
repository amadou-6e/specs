# S-002 — AneuriskData

## Material

- Canonical URL: [AneuriskData repository](https://github.com/permfl/AneuriskData)
- Source type: repository mirror of medical geometry, volumes, and centerlines
- Evidence class: primary artifact mirror
- Accessed: not uniformly recorded in the originating survey
- Rights: CC BY-NC 3.0 reported by the originating survey

## Relevance and claims used

The repository exposes per-case STL/VTP surfaces and VTP/CSV centerlines, plus image
and label material useful for geometry/path studies.

## Material inspected

Repository structure and representative surface and centerline paths were reviewed.

## Limitations

The inspected documentation did not explicitly establish the centerline coordinate
frame. The mirror is not a standardized path-planning benchmark and supplies no
canonical start/goal task set.

# S-007 — SynthAorta

## Material

- Canonical links: [dataset record](https://repository.tugraz.at/records/ft6av-k4w55), [source repository](https://github.com/domagoj-bosnjak/SynthAorta)
- Source type: synthetic aorta dataset and generator source
- Evidence class: primary
- Accessed: not uniformly recorded in the originating survey
- Rights: GPL-3.0-or-later reported for the code; data rights require verification

## Relevance and claims used

The source supplies synthetic structured meshes and centerlines. It can complement
real surface datasets but does not directly supply the required STL representation.

## Material inspected

The dataset and repository descriptions were reviewed by the originating survey.

## Limitations

Surface extraction and STL conversion create derived artifacts. Synthetic-to-real
transfer, coordinate preservation, generator identity, and dataset rights require
explicit treatment.

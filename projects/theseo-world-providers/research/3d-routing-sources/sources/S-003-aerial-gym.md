## Relevance and inspected material

The source tree contains bounded obstacle configurations, URDF collision assets,
seeded placement logic, navigation tasks, and sensor observations. Sample collision
definitions and the environment asset tree were inspected.

## Claims used

- It is a candidate compact-flight training source.
- Exporting geometry into Theseo produces a derived task distinct from native scores.

## Limitations

Obstacle count alone does not characterize topology. Poses, collision transforms,
start/goal sampling, licensing, and derived-task validity remain to be audited.

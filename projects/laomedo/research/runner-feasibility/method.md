# Retrospective feasibility method

Organization of existing reports, not newly performed source discovery. Original issue/
protocol conditions and controls remain in the preserved records. The migration inspected
local documents only, without upstream re-verification, private-artifact access or model calls.

Sources came from the local feasibility inventory at specs revision 2854612. Classify local
observation, published upstream claim, synthetic check and inference separately. Compare
runtime pins/tasks before comparing results. Preserve Windows/Docker/version contradictions
rather than explain them by guessing. No prospective gate, timestamp, hardware measurement
or independent replication is invented. Source records state identities and access limits;
private evidence unavailable to a specs reader must not be fabricated. Atomic findings are
cross-source authoring summaries, not replacement results.

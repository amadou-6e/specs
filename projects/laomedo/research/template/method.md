# Snapshot identity method example

Illustrative method: inspect a published pilot for native thread identity, post-run
hash checks and rejection timing. Distinguish reported observation from independent
reproduction. Compare fresh/resume on one pinned implementation. Record unavailable
raw artifacts as a limitation. Copying this example does not inspect private evidence,
invoke a model or reproduce the pilot. A real copy records its inspected sources and pins.

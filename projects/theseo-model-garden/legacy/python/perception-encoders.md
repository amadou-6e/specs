# Relocated perception-encoder research

> This entry is historical and is not an authoritative specification.

## Archived material

This compatibility record preserves the former location used to identify the Model
Garden's broad perception-encoder research topic. It does not contain a frozen
experiment protocol or empirical result; those remain separate, identity-bearing
legacy records where applicable.

## Why it was archived

The single-file topic was replaced by a structured research entry that separates its
question, method, source registry, and individual findings. Active experiment
protocols and results are owned by structured entries under `experiments/`, rather
than by this relocation pointer.

was not recorded in this compatibility record. New work must use the current owners
linked below.

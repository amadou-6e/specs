# Campaign protocol and inherited sub-protocol register

## Authority model

This record was reconstructed after the campaign ran. It is therefore not a
retroactively frozen campaign-wide protocol. The common campaign boundary is the
compact small-field encoder and its independently evaluated geometry or collision
readouts. Each authorized study retained its own frozen protocol, identities, gates,
and budget. The iteration records in this entry state the resulting protocol lineage;
the linked legacy documents preserve the original text and commit-era evidence.

The proposed [optimization campaign](../../legacy/python/perception-encoder-compact-optimization.md)
is a planning source. It does not retroactively govern studies that were frozen under
other documents.

## Common controls

- Keep protocol, implementation, dataset, model, and report identities explicit.
- Separate encoder training from independent readout evaluation.
- Preserve null, raw-input, and stronger spatial controls where required by the
  inherited protocol.
- Keep test and OOD evidence out of recipe selection unless the applicable protocol
  explicitly permits that use.
- Preserve failed gates and post-hoc diagnoses as such; never average a failing seed
  into a pass or reinterpret a diagnostic as qualification.
- Treat package creation as an experimental artifact, not deployment approval.

## Ordered protocol lineage

| Iteration family | Inherited sub-protocols | Authorized purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Engineering screens | smoke, controls, learnability, dense-output | Establish execution, control, and learnability preconditions. |
| Optimization | search, diagnosis, diversity, nonlinear readout, reconstruction, structured code | Search and diagnose compact architectures under successively authorized rules. |
| Qualification | replication, robustness/collision transfer, package, validation assessment | Replicate the retained recipe and test bounded transfer and robustness claims. |
| Collision readout | frozen collision-readout follow-up | Compare revised heads on the fixed 729-value code under fresh data and locked calibration. |

## Decision logic

Every sub-protocol owns its own thresholds. A later positive result cannot erase an
earlier failed gate. The terminal campaign disposition follows the collision-readout
protocol: retain revised geometry-aware readouts if they pass the ID improvement
screen, but do not promote unless the stronger raw/spatial competitiveness screen is
also met. The latter gate failed in every seed.

## Permitted adaptation

New protocol documents could change architectures, datasets, or readouts only as an
explicit successor study. Frozen encoder, normalization, and package identities were
required wherever a readout-only study claimed to isolate the head. Any future work
must use fresh authorization and must not tune against the locked evidence registered
here.

# Compact perception-encoder campaign

## Problem statement

Determine whether a compact frozen representation can retain useful local geometry
and support independent readouts under bounded robustness and collision tests.

## Migration boundary

This is a historical campaign aggregate, not one experiment executed under one
unchanged frozen protocol. The work advanced through separately authorized
sub-protocols as earlier evidence answered or narrowed the next question. The four
iteration families below preserve that chronology and disclose their inherited
sub-protocols. The local records are the canonical navigational and interpretive
specification for the campaign; the immutable legacy documents remain provenance for
the original wording and identities.

## Research questions

1. Can a compact small-field encoder preserve enough local geometry for useful
   independent readouts?
2. Which architecture and training changes improve compact-code detail without
   invalidating the controls used to interpret it?
3. Do retained compact encoders replicate and remain useful under robustness and
   collision-transfer tests?
4. Can a revised collision readout recover information from the fixed 729-value code
   without adapting the encoder?

## Scope and non-claims

The campaign covers synthetic small-field geometry, compact-code qualification, and
bounded collision readouts. It does not establish deployment safety, global routing
quality, RL navigation performance, real-sensor robustness, or superiority under a
single preregistered campaign-wide comparison. Later sub-protocols may use earlier
reports as motivation, but may not retroactively change earlier gates.

## Status and summarized outcome

Status: `complete`.

The campaign produced a retained 729-value compact code and geometry-aware readout
directions. The final collision-readout study improved over the original bounded
head on its preregistered ID screen, but did not meet competitiveness gates against
raw-grid and spatial controls. The disposition is retain without promotion.

# Qualification results

The robustness study did not support broad distributional robustness. Sparse density,
visible noise, and contiguous missing slabs caused widespread failures; the seven-
slice slab failed every seed/family group. Baseline also exposed failures on unseen
gyroid geometry.

Collision transfer showed useful compact-code signal: ID AUPRC was 0.9010-0.9055
across seeds, far above null and shuffled controls. However, raw-grid and spatial
controls achieved 0.9929-0.9946 and 0.9888-0.9897, so every compact seed failed the
frozen 0.03 competitiveness margins. Uncertain-path analysis further showed that
operating-point behavior and ranking must remain distinct.

Disposition: retain experimental evidence and the package, with no promotion. This
does not establish deployment, global topology, finite-body collision, navigation,
or real-sensor qualification. The failed robustness and competitiveness gates remain
in force and motivated the separately frozen collision-readout successor.

# Optimization execution

## Protocol lineage

1. `compact-search-v1` opened the bounded 48 GPU-hour campaign with fresh
   train/probe/selection/development parents, fixed 33-to-17 geometry, independent
   readouts, and an eight-hour first-tranche cap.
2. The diagnosis measured the completed search after results were known; its findings
   are post-hoc and only authorized later work.
3. Diversity/detail training tested six recipes under fresh identities.
4. Nonlinear independent readouts tested whether probe structure, rather than the
   compact code alone, explained observed limitations.
5. Reconstruction training and spatially structured-code search were separately
   frozen successors with their own controls and gates.

All execution identities, checkpoints, reports, and deviations are registered in the
[evidence manifest](evidence/README.md). The studies share a campaign budget and
technical boundary, but their distinct protocols prevent a single pooled comparison.

# Encoder-continuation results

At matched 192-geometry probe exposure, boundary F1 improved from
0.6343/0.6129/0.6239 to 0.7380/0.7328/0.7374. The paired log-loss improvement 95% CI
was [0.04678, 0.06215]. The registered direction passed, retaining the new encoders as
local-geometry candidates for distance retention and held-out-family validation. The
pretraining bundle changed several factors, so the result does not isolate one cause.

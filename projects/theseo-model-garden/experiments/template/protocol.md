# Frozen protocol

State whether this protocol was frozen before decision-bearing observations. A
retrospective reconstruction must say so and must not use the word "frozen" as an
authority claim.

## Questions, hypotheses, and variables

Record bounded questions, falsifiable hypotheses, independent variables, dependent
variables, controls, and baselines.

## Populations and splits

Define disjoint data, world, geometry, route, and task populations; sampling rules;
seeds; sample sizes; and leakage checks. State the unit across which generalization is
claimed.

## Metrics and analysis

Define primary and secondary metrics, populations, aggregation, uncertainty,
comparisons, thresholds, and analysis methods.

## Stop and decision gates

For every gate, specify its evaluation point, exact evidence, pass/fail/unevaluable
rule, required disposition, and whether another iteration is permitted.

## Iteration and adaptation rules

Define planned sub-experiments, iteration order, permitted adaptations, rerun policy,
and forbidden post-hoc changes. Changes outside these rules require a new prospective
protocol revision or a separately authorized experiment.

## Execution identity and resources

Pin governing spec, code, dependency, configuration, input, checkpoint, and artifact
identity requirements. State commands, hardware assumptions, resource budget, and
retention expectations.

## Threats and non-claims

Record known confounds, external-validity limits, determinism limits, and conclusions
the experiment cannot support.
